# The close

For the end of [priority-deepdive](../SKILL.md), when the work on one priority ends, either because
it is finished or because they stop. It has gone well when someone could work from the section, as
far as the conversation got, without the strategy misleading them.

1. **Read the section through as its reader would.** It should read as one piece and end with its
   next actions, each saying whose it is, because a section edited as you went can repeat or
   contradict itself. It answers the four questions in the order steps 3 to 6 take them, whatever
   order it had before, because each rests on the one before; `node
   "$PLUGIN_ROOT/skills/quality-strategy/scripts/records.mjs" check <strategy.md>` reports a part
   that is missing or out of order.
2. **Read the rest of the document for what this work changed elsewhere,** such as the bottom line,
   the priorities table, another section, or a profile that states the same bar, and fix it in the
   same save, because a worker may take any part away and act on it. Rewrite the bottom line as one
   paragraph that is true now, rather than adding to it, because the decision log keeps what it said
   before and a reader acts on the paragraph they find.
3. **If they stopped,** write what is left unanswered in the section once, rather than putting it
   to them now, so that it is neither lost nor read as theirs. Write a choice that is theirs to make
   as one of the section's open questions, and a proposal of yours as an item under that question,
   saying that it is yours and whether you put it to them.
4. **Have it reviewed by a reader who did not write it,** because a plan you rewrote as the
   conversation went can contradict itself in ways you read past. Run the review as the last
   check in [the write-up's review](../../connect-quality-strategy/references/review.md) describes,
   including what makes the reader fresh, over this priority's section and the other parts of the
   document this work changed: the same brief, the same handling of what comes back, and the same
   course where your harness offers no fresh agent.
5. **Write one decision log entry** for this priority in this sitting: what changed in the strategy
   and whose change it is, in a few lines, as [what is true
   now](../../../references/templates/every-document.md#what-is-true-now) says, rather than
   retelling the sitting, because the section already says what is true now.
6. **Hand back** as [stopping and coming back](../../../references/stopping-and-coming-back.md)
   says, which keeps the progress note and commits the records. If they have not seen the plan as
   written, even one they accepted before it was written, give it in full, as your suggestion, which
   they can change; otherwise say only what changed. Say in a line or two what the review found that
   you changed and what needs them; or that no other reader reviewed it, with the line that review
   gives for that case. Offer nothing else, because they can ask for a fresh write-up or a review of
   the whole document when they want one.
