# Assess quality gaps: put the findings to the user

This is how the findings go to the user, and what to do with their reply.

1. Outside a whole strategy, write the top of the finding for its reader, as [a result outside a
   strategy](../../../references/writing-your-part.md#a-result-outside-a-strategy) describes,
   saying that the user has not yet seen it. Open with the judgment (Above bar, At bar, Below bar,
   or Unknown and why) before what any source claims, because the reader takes the first word as
   the answer. A judgment is only as strong as what was seen: one that would need "if this claim
   holds" in front of it is Unknown, naming the claim, because a conditional Below bar reads as
   Below bar. What the documents describe cannot meet or miss a bar about what actually happens.
   An Unknown says what could settle it, as [a good
   record](while-investigating.md#a-good-record) asks. Then run `comms-review` on it and deal with what it
   finds. In a whole strategy the connecting step does this once, at the end.
2. Put the findings to the user whole, in one message, before any work is planned. Wait until every
   planned check that runs, as [where to start](where-to-start.md) sorts them, has run, by its own
   invocation or another's, or been declined, so that they read the findings once. A check that
   waits does not hold the findings back: its row's finding says what it waits on. A fetch or an
   install is asked for first, as [while investigating](while-investigating.md#as-each-finding-is-made)
   says. Name each check with no run of its own in the findings, with the run that showed it, so
   that they can ask for it on its own, and don't report it as a run of its own. Name a check as
   shown only by a run that has shown it. If a stop comes first, show what was found so far and
   record the pass as started, as [record and close](record-and-close.md) says.

   When a finding rests on something this assessment ran, its [evidence
   folder](assessment-notes.md) keeps the commands. A next
   observation of "run these again" helps only if someone can. So before this message, run the
   commands once exactly as the folder gives them, from where it says, into a fresh empty folder, so
   that the commands kept are the ones that ran. Type every path as the folder writes it, relative
   ones included, because that is what the owner will type; a recipe that needs an absolute path
   says so. A recipe that does not run as written is corrected,
   or the finding says it could not be rerun. A pre-read folder reused unchanged is linked, not
   rerun; rerun it only when something has changed, as [what to bring](what-to-bring.md) says.

   Say which rows were assessed and what was looked at. The rows in scope are the ones the
   measurement plan covers, as its scope sets them; name the rest together with their ratings, as
   not checked before this release, because a row left out without a word reads as forgotten.
   Something seen in a row out of scope is still reported, because a known fault matters whatever
   the row's rating. Then, in this order:
   - where each row stands, with what most changes the release call first, whether that is a
     fault or an unknown; each unknown with why it is unknown and what would find it out, or that
     no way is known yet;
   - the bottom line, what it all adds up to, named as the agent's reading until they answer;
   - after it, once and briefly, what else was noticed, as the assessment notes hold it, because
     they cannot act on what they do not see.

   Say which questions the findings leave open or have sharpened, each once with how it could be
   found out, or "not yet worked out", as [the Open
   guidance](../../../references/open-section.md) says. Name nobody for the user to ask, because
   how they find out is theirs. One way is for the agent to look further on their behalf, where
   they allow it.

   Where a finding needs a judgment or a fact only they can give, such as whether they already do
   something some other way, ask it here, as part of the findings, because the finding's confidence
   depends on it. Propose no work and no next step, because that is the plan of work's.
3. End that message so that they know what, if anything, is theirs to answer, and wait. A question
   is worth their turn only if its answer could change the judgment. Where they stated the bar, said
   what would settle it and how, or said they will give it later, they have answered already: the
   finding records what they said, and a good ending is the finding with an invitation to correct
   it. A bar they stated is answered even where a word in it can be read more than one way. Where
   the judgment would differ by reading, the finding gives it under each, and their correction
   settles which. Where it would not, leave the word alone, because asking what it means asks again
   for a bar they have given, and the answer could not change the judgment. Where a judgment or a fact only they can give would change a finding, such as a bar they
   have not stated, ask that one.
4. When they reply:
   - Correct what they correct, and say what that changes.
   - If they mention something they have seen or tried themselves, and what it showed could change
     a finding, ask what it showed. Add it to the rows it bears on, with who saw it, because their
     own observation is evidence, not a caveat on the agent's. Where the records already hold it, or
     it bears on no row in scope, say so instead of asking, because an answer that changes nothing
     costs them a turn.
   - If they dispute a finding without correcting a fact, keep the finding and note what they said
     beside it.
5. Mark what they saw as `seen and answered`, with the date. Their answer settles the bottom line as
   theirs, or as the agent's reading they have seen. Outside a whole strategy, bring the top of the
   finding up to date in the same save, because the file must not state as settled what they have
   since corrected.

When they have answered, or when a stop came first, open [record and close](record-and-close.md)
now, because that is where the pass is recorded and the session closed.
