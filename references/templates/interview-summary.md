# The interview summary

`interviews/<sitting>/quality-interview-summary.md`: what was on someone's mind about the
project's quality, in their words, written at the end of a sitting from its transcript. Later steps
check the strategy against it. What every document shares is in [every document](every-document.md).

```markdown
# <title>

## What was on their mind

<in their words, quoted from the transcript, with no levels or ratings they did not give>

## Topics

<each topic touched on, with the quotes it rests on and pointers to the transcript turns they come
from, and what else the sitting gave of it: a thought with nothing beyond its quote is one line.
Why it matters, in their words, or the question it was said in answer to; "not said" only where a
reader would otherwise supply a reason, because a reason the agent supplies reads as theirs. The
risks and worries they named, called out as theirs and not sorted into levels. How interested they
seemed, where something showed it, marked as the interviewer's impression, which is not their
rating>

## Still open

<what was not reached or not settled: questions asked and not answered, questions they declined
(with any limit they gave, such as "not tonight"), and the agent's own questions that were not
reached, saying which of these each one is>
```
