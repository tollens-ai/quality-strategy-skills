# A stakeholder's profile

`stakeholders/<slug>.md`: the user's understanding of one stakeholder or group, which the priorities
step later sets beside the other profiles. What a bar and its levels mean is in [the quality
model](../quality-model.md#ratings-and-bars); what every document shares is in [every
document](every-document.md).

```markdown
# <the stakeholder>

<who they are: their role, how they relate to the product, anything in their situation that changes
what they need (such as their device, or how and when the result reaches them), and who in the
group differs from the rest. Then, once for the whole profile, whose account this is and what it is
based on, such as "the owner's own view; they run the weekly support rota" or "the owner's
understanding, from the support inbox; nobody in this group has been asked". Then how thorough it
is: a full view, or a quick pass the user chose, and for a quick pass, whether they want to come
back to it in full and what they said about when>

## What matters to them, and how good it must be

<for each thing that matters to them, the product or part and the quality dimension, so that two
stakeholders' views of the same thing can be joined later. Then, in the user's words wherever they
gave them: what would be good enough, what would make them say "ugh", what would be unacceptable,
and what would delight them. Keep "ugh" and unacceptable apart only where the user drew the line
between them. Add the conditions, or how much or how often, where that changes the answer. Record
what the user has seen for themselves, saying that it is their own observation. Where the user did
not give one of these, write "not asked" or "could not say", with the question. If the user says
something does not matter to this stakeholder, keep it here with their reason, so that nobody asks
again and the release's non-goals can draw on it>

## What comes first for them

<what comes first for them and why, as the user understands it, in their words, and what matters
less; "not yet said" for what they did not reach. No scale and no rating per item>

## Open questions about them

<the user's questions about this stakeholder, each with why it matters and how the user could find
out, or "no way chosen yet"; tensions with other stakeholders' views>
```

Groups need not mirror a hierarchy: a group can link to its members' profiles, and someone in two
groups is one stakeholder with one profile. Facts about a stakeholder's situation that more than one
session needs live in the project context, linked from here.
