# The project context

`project-context.md`: what more than one session needs to know about the project and the release.
Most of it cannot be read from the repository: what the project is for, what the release must
achieve and who matters are the user's to say. What every document shares is in [every
document](every-document.md).

```markdown
# Project context: <the product>

## What it is and why

<what is being built, for whom, and the ambition behind it, in the user's words where they gave
them; the products, tools or other things whose quality is in question>

## How work is done and checked

<who does the work, people and agents, how it is checked now, and where that goes well or badly>

## How a release reaches people

<how something built becomes something people use: building, shipping, installing, running>

## Limits

<what bounds the work: time, people, money, platforms; the facts about the product that bring an
obligation (such as holding personal information); and the user's rules on how the quality work is
done, such as what may or may not be run, contacted or changed, because every next action in the
strategy has to fit within them. Each once, with what the user said about it>

## This release

<what this release is for and what it covers, in the user's words>

## Who matters

<the stakeholders who matter for this release, in the order the user wants to take them, with how
each relates to the product, facts about their situation, and the concerns raised so far, in the
words of whoever raised them and saying who that was. A note meant for a particular stakeholder's
deep dive names the stakeholder it is for>
```
