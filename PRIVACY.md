# Privacy

Quality Strategy Skills, published by Tollens, is a set of instructions for your own AI agent, with
two small scripts that keep its records on your machine. It has no server of its own.

- **What Tollens collects:** nothing. The pack does not send your conversations, your files or any
  usage data to Tollens, and its scripts make no network requests.
- **What the pack saves:** the documents it writes with you, such as interview records, stakeholder
  views and the strategy, as plain text files in a folder you choose. They stay where you put them.
  If that folder is in a shared repository, whoever can read the repository can read them once you
  commit and push.
- **Your agent:** your conversation with the agent, and any files it reads, are handled by the
  company that provides it (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) under your agreement with them. The
  pack does not change that.
- **GitHub:** anything you post in the project's
  [issues](https://github.com/tollens-ai/quality-strategy-skills/issues) is public. Share only
  material suitable for a public issue.
